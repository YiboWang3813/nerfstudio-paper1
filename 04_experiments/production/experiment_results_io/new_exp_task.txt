十七、最终论文叙事目标
======================================================================

Joint:
三大模块是 complementary roles，不是三个 accuracy booster。

PPP:
核心 claim 是：
acquisition regularity can replace measured-pose geometry while maintaining coherent reconstruction.

LGR:
核心 claim 是：
ultrasound rendering should progressively account for:
- finite elevational slice
- depth-adaptive slice response
- preceding-path propagation
- explicit causal ray interaction
- decoupled echo/transmission attributes

最终 full LGR 应对应最新 Methods，而不是旧历史开关。

CGA:
核心 claim 是：
residual-guided densification allocates capacity toward under-reconstructed regions;
local/path-aware pruning controls the resulting population while preserving rendering-relevant primitives.

Hyperparameter:
核心 claim 不是“default 是所有数据绝对最优”，而是：
the selected defaults lie in stable operating regions and generalize across synthetic and real data without per-sequence tuning.
